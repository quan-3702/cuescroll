CueScroll – How to use

A teleprompter: your text scrolls slowly up the screen while you read it aloud – for videos, speeches and online meetings.

1. Write or import your text

  1. Open CueScroll. Your scripts are listed on the left.
  2. Click + New to start a new script.
  3. Type a title in the top box, then type or paste your text into the large box. It is saved automatically.
  4. Already have the text in a file? Click Import .txt and choose a plain text file.

The line under the text shows how many words you have written. To remove a script, open it and click Delete script.

2. Start reading

  1. Click ▶ Start prompter. The screen turns dark and shows your text in large letters.
  2. If you set a Countdown, wait for it to finish. Otherwise press Space to start.
  3. Read along the red guide line near the top. The text moves up by itself.
  4. Press Space to pause and to continue.
  5. Press Esc or click ✕ to go back to your script.

3. Keys and buttons while reading

  • Space – play / pause
  • ↑ ↓ – faster / slower
  • ← → – jump back / forward a little
  • F – full screen on / off
  • Esc – leave the prompter

Move the mouse to show the control bar at the bottom: ▶ play, ⟲ back to the start, − + speed, A− A+ letter size, ⇋ mirror, ⛶ full screen. The bar hides again when you stop moving the mouse.

4. Set the speed and the look

Use the panel on the right before you start:

  • Speed – how fast the text moves. Start slow; most people read best a little slower than they expect.
  • Countdown before start – 3, 5 or 10 seconds to get ready.
  • Font size, Text width and Line spacing – larger letters are easier to read from a distance; a narrower text keeps your eyes from moving sideways.
  • Align – left or centered.
  • Colors – white, yellow or green on black, or black on white.
  • Reading guide line – shows or hides the red line.

5. Look into the camera

Put the CueScroll window as close to your webcam as you can, make the text narrow, and sit a little further back. Your eyes then stay near the lens and it looks as if you are speaking freely.

Mirror horizontally and Flip vertically are only for teleprompter glass (a beam-splitter in front of a camera), where the text is seen in a mirror. Leave them off for normal use.

6. Use it during a video call – floating window (Pro)

  1. Start the prompter and click ⧉ Float.
  2. A small window with your text opens and stays on top of other programs such as Zoom, Teams or your camera app.
  3. Drag it right under your webcam and make it as wide as you like.
  4. Close the small window when you are done.

7. Record yourself with the webcam (Pro)

  1. Start the prompter and click ● Record.
  2. Allow CueScroll to use your camera and microphone when Windows asks.
  3. You see yourself behind the text. Read your script.
  4. Click the record button again to stop. The video is saved in your Downloads folder.

The text is only on your screen. It is not in the recorded video.

8. Free version and Pro

The free version includes the full prompter with all speed, text and display settings, and keeps up to 2 scripts. Pro is a one-time purchase and adds webcam recording, the floating window and unlimited scripts. Click ★ Get Pro at the top to see the price, or Restore purchase if you bought it before.

9. If something goes wrong

  • The text moves too fast or too slow: press ↓ or ↑ while reading.
  • The camera does not start: close other programs that use the camera, and check Windows Settings → Privacy → Camera.
  • My scripts are gone: scripts are stored on this PC in this app. They do not move to another PC, and clearing the app data removes them. Keep important texts in a file as well.
