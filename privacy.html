CueScroll – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

CueScroll works entirely on your device. Your scripts and settings are stored locally on your PC and are never uploaded to any server.

If you use the optional recording feature, the app asks for access to your camera and microphone. The video is recorded and saved locally on your PC; it is never sent anywhere.

We do not collect, store or share any personal data. We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
